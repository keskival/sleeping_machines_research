# Proposal to the founding partners: capital, co-founder equity and team reserve

**Non-binding proposal, 7 October 2026.** From Tero Keski-Valkama, founder, to the prospective investor-partners (the
"Partners"). It replaces the initial offer of €200,000 for a 20% stake. Binding terms follow only in a term sheet and
shareholders' agreement (*pacto de socios*) drafted by Spanish startup counsel. Figures assume two Partners; §7 shows one
and three.

## 1. Summary

| | Initial offer | This proposal |
|---|---|---|
| Capital | €200,000 | €200,000 in new shares at **€15M pre-money**, fully diluted |
| Co-founder work (go-to-market, investor relations, operations) | Inside the 20% | **3.0% per Partner** as founding shares with reverse vesting: 4 years, 1-year cliff, partly milestone-linked |
| Founder salary bridge | Inside the 20% | Priced like money: converts into shares at the same price (§5) |
| Team reserve | — | **10%**: 3.0% earmarked for Karoliina Salminen on the same terms as the Partners, 7.0% option pool for future employees |
| **Partners' total (two)** | **20% immediately** | **7.3% fully diluted**, of which 6.0% is earned over four years |

The proposal separates three things that the initial offer priced as one: cash, future work and the salary bridge. Each is
priced on its own terms, and each Partner's stake grows with what they deliver.

## 2. Why €15M pre-money

- **Public evidence:**
  - Confirmed wins over the best published results on all five EasyTPP leaderboard datasets (Taxi, Taobao, StackOverflow,
    Retweet, Amazon),
    StackOverflow at matched model size and compute.
  - A second domain: P19 sepsis prediction, AUROC 0.916 vs 0.903 published.
  - The Taxi win reproduced from scratch on separate hardware (0.5252 vs 0.5250), with a one-command kit ready for a
    third-party rerun.

  Most pre-seed companies raising €200,000 have no public benchmark result at all.
- **Reference point:** the valuation rationale (VALUATION_RATIONALE.md) puts the institutional-round target at €50M and
  higher. €15M is the price for an early, small, founder-friendly round that rewards the Partners for helping close that
  round.
- **The Partners' real cost is lower than the headline:** a subscription of new shares should qualify for the Spanish
  personal income-tax deduction for investment in new companies (§6). At 50%, €200,000 costs about €100,000 after tax, the
  same net price as paying the full amount at about a €7.5M pre-money valuation.

## 4. Vesting: Partners and Karoliina on the same terms

Each co-founder grant of 3.0% vests in two parts:

| Part | Share of grant | Vests on |
|---|---|---|
| Time | 60% (1.8%) | 4 years, monthly, 1-year cliff; it continues while the person works for the company in the agreed role |
| Milestones | 40% (1.2%) | Two milestones from the role's list below, 0.6% each, within 36 months |

**Milestones: Partners (go-to-market and investor relations)**
1. An institutional round of at least €3M closed at a pre-money valuation of at least €30M.
2. At least €500,000 non-dilutive funding awarded (EIC Accelerator, CDTI/NEOTEC, ENISA or regional).
3. Two paid design-partner pilots signed (EIC TRL-6 path, `experiments/pilot/`).
4. A first commercial contract of at least €100,000 per year.

**Milestones: Karoliina Salminen (research and engineering)**
1. A third-party reproduction of a leaderboard result, or a paper accepted at a peer-reviewed venue.
2. A new confirmed public-benchmark win, or an integrated-model result that passes a declared research gate.
3. A production-grade inference or training component adopted in a pilot.
4. A patent application filed with Karoliina as inventor.

The research and engineering roles get different milestones but the same structure, size and schedule. If Karoliina joins
after incorporation, the earmarked 3.0% is granted from the team reserve as options under Ley 28/2022, with vesting
counted from the joining date. Karoliina's existing research credit is recognized separately (IP_PROTECTION_PLAN.md) and
does not depend on this grant.

**Common terms for all co-founder grants:**
- *Reverse vesting:* unvested shares are repurchased at nominal value when the person leaves.
- *Good leaver* (illness, termination without cause): keeps vested shares; vested shares may be bought at fair value.
- *Bad leaver* (breach, competing activity, termination for cause): vested shares are repurchased at the lower of nominal
  and fair value.
- *Acceleration:* double trigger. On a sale of the company followed by termination without cause, all unvested shares vest.
- *Obligations:* full assignment of work IP to the company, confidentiality, and a non-compete during the vesting period.

**Optional performance top-up** (if the Partners ask for more than 3% each): warrants for up to an additional 1.5% per
Partner, exercisable only after Partner milestone 1 above. With the top-up, the Partners can earn up to 10.3% together,
compared with 20% unconditionally in the initial offer.

## 5. Founder salary bridge

The bridge is cash for the company's benefit, so it is priced like the €200,000:

- **Recommended:** the Partners pay the bridge into the S.L., which contracts the founder directly. Amounts paid convert
  into shares at the €15M pre-money price at the next capital increase (*compensación de créditos*). The paid amount is
  capped at an agreed monthly figure for up to 12 months.
- **As offered (part paid through Pataluha Ventures):** possible only with these conditions:
  1. Pataluha Ventures signs, before any payment, a written waiver and assignment confirming that it has no rights in the
     company's models, code, data, patents or results, and that the founder's work for the company is not Pataluha work.
  2. Each amount the Partners fund is documented monthly and converts into shares at the same price.
  3. The arrangement ends when the company can pay the founder itself or at the institutional round, whichever is first.

The bridge pays the founder's salary. It is not consideration for the Partners' co-founder shares, which are earned by
vesting (§4).

## 6. Tax and legal points for counsel

- **Investor deduction:** confirm the personal income-tax deduction for investment in new or recently created companies
  (LIRPF art. 68.1, as amended by Ley 28/2022) for the €200,000:
  - 50% of up to €100,000 per investor per year;
  - company age and own-funds limits;
  - holding period;
  - the 40% shareholding limit with relatives;
  - whether a co-founder who holds a management role remains eligible.
- **Founding shares at nominal:** shares received for services are taxable at market value. Issuing the co-founder shares
  at incorporation, before the priced round, keeps their value at nominal. Counsel should confirm this, or use options
  under Ley 28/2022, which allows tax deferral and an exemption of up to €50,000 per year for employees of a certified
  startup.
- **Startup certification:** apply to ENISA for certification as an *empresa emergente* at incorporation. The tax regime
  and the option rules depend on it.
- **ENISA participative loans:** they are sized against the company's equity (*fondos propios*). The €200,000 in equity and
  the share premium raise that base.
- **IP chain of title:**
  - the Pataluha waiver (§5);
  - the founder's assignment of the research and code to the S.L.;
  - documentation of all contributions (IP_PROTECTION_PLAN.md);
  - the founder's earlier patents, which belong to HERE Global B.V. and are excluded from the company's assets.

## 7. Governance and investor rights

- **Board:** the founder holds the majority of seats until the institutional round. The Partners hold one seat together
  while they are co-founders, otherwise an observer seat.
- **Reserved matters:** these need the founder's and the Partners' consent: new share classes, sale of the company, a
  change of business, and related-party transactions.
- **Investor rights:**
  - quarterly information;
  - pro-rata participation in the next round for their capital stake;
  - a most-favoured-nation clause for any other investor in this round;
  - tag-along;
  - drag-along at a majority including the founder.
- **No liquidation preference above 1× non-participating** in this round.
