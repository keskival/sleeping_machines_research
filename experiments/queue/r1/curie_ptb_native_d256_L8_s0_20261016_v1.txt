# Battle R1, published word-level benchmark PTB, development round 1 (validation only). Decision: first native development number against the published static (Mogrifier 50.1, AWD-LSTM 54.4/58.8) and dynamic-evaluation (44.8) columns; the learning curve (train vs validation by epoch) decides round 2: overfitting -> regularisation, underfitting -> width/depth (AWS slots), and whether online adaptation's gain matches dynamic evaluation's 5-7 perplexity.
# Stationarity/online: published fixed-split benchmark (Mikolov PTB, Melis et al. 2020 Table 1 static 50.1 / dynamic-evaluation 44.8 at 24M). Both columns are scored: frozen weights (static column) and causal online weight adaptation on already-scored validation text (dynamic-evaluation column); test stays sealed until a selected model is scored once.
# Fighter rationale: plan-B deep keyed temporal-memory member, the composition where depth pays (FineWeb one pass 4M: L8 5.8414 vs L2 5.8648, gap growing with data; AWS 1M ladder monotone to L16), widened to d=256 for a 10k-word vocabulary (9M parameters); its output keyed token-slot bonus is a learned cache pointer trained end to end, and causal online adaptation is native, the two mechanisms behind the published leaders.
# State/history: persistent temporal memory, per-layer keyed race reads and keyed slots over each 512-token training lane (full backprop over the lane); scoring windows of 512 tokens advanced by 128 (every target sees 384-511 tokens of history across sentence and article boundaries); online arm adapts weights every 64 scored targets (RMS-normalised step, decay toward trained weights).
# Retained: decaying/rotating temporal memory, sparse token-slot keyed writes, separate keys/values, race-attention reads per layer, online adaptation. Not included: hard routes/counterfactual route credit (full-backprop member); temporal memory state beyond the 512-token window at scoring (window recompute; exact carried-state scoring is the next implementation step). Restrictions: none imposed for symmetry; d=256/L8 is the largest member a single Curie core trains in ~8 h at 16 epochs (first development round).
