# Evidence-led synthesis for the next native compositional learner

16 October 2026. R1/G-HIER. This synthesis governs new construction, not changes to active source-bound runs. No new training admission or benchmark claim follows from this note.

## Decision

Build from the existing versioned native hierarchy and its race-based multi-memory interfaces, integrating the demonstrated addressed-recall path and local receiver eligibility. Do not describe the current native language learner as the strongest available compositional integration: it omits mechanisms already implemented in the hierarchy. Its 64K acquisition tests an implementation and learning foundation, not the family's competitive ceiling. No new architecture or acquisition is admitted until the mechanism mapping below is resolved into an explicit integration contract.

The evidence selects three different engineering jobs: make information recoverable, make joint relations learnable, and provide enough diverse data to acquire them. These are complementary. A correctness contract is not a quality result; a budget-limited failure is not an impossibility theorem.

## Scale: distinguish opportunities, examples and independent structure

The composition continuation used 2048 unique scenes, but only 64 abstract TRAIN arrangements with new nuisance/geometry tapes. More tapes teach robustness and identity; they do not add new abstract relation combinations. Fresh DEV tapes test nuisance transfer, not a new combinatorial training distribution. Both the arrangement coverage and the optimization budget remain possible limits. Near-chance TRAIN fitting localizes an acquisition difficulty but does not identify whether its cause is data support, conditioning, stochastic credit, objective, or computation.

The 64K native language acquisition is far below the saved 4M/16M diagnostic fitting budgets. Its final DEV can establish acquisition progress and identify gross defects. It cannot establish a competitive family loss. Upper levels may also receive fewer independent useful compositions than raw token count suggests. Count distinct identities, arrangements, contexts, exposures and actual emissions per level, not just tokens or presentations.

Classify findings explicitly:

- **Measured implementation defect:** ownership misses 25% of complete groups; same-role collisions; illegal time/version aliasing; top-to-bottom feedback causing instability. Correctness repairs do not require waiting for more data.
- **Failure at tested budget/distribution:** whole-relation acquisition, limited native depth quality, and short local fits. Keep insufficient data/coverage/optimization as live explanations.
- **Restricted mathematical impossibility:** fixed separate-child additive features plus an affine logistic head cannot solve the balanced parity construction. The actual nonlinear actor is outside that restricted proof. Scaling may teach its interactions.

Adequate scale means a declared distribution and optimization plan with exact recovery, not an unchanged arbitrary extension. First reuse completed checkpoints and inspect training/coverage trajectories. Where structure coverage is limited, broaden legal TRAIN combinations while preserving held-out combinations and TEST. Where fitting is still improving, continuation can be more informative than replacement. Do not promise that a fixed budget will isolate a cause.

## Integrated construction and admission obligations

1. **Persistent objects, not just saved events.** Reuse indexed native objects, publication versions, directories and causal read/transport. Use the same object interface at every depth. Preserve an evolving local state alongside recoverable parts. Distinguish instance, publication version, semantic identity and notification identity.
2. **Native assemblies inside higher layers.** A higher memory queries several eligible lower memories and consumes their responses through its existing native races. Preserve identity, role/order and availability. Joint operations already exist; migrate and test them rather than reinventing them. Generalize geometric role metadata to causal language order/source metadata without hidden semantic labels.
3. **Complementary access paths.** Keep the demonstrated direct addressed prediction path while assemblies learn. Output-head access does not substitute for higher-layer access. Different rates are allowed, but a slower clock is not proof of abstraction, and extra depth can be useful by making the same information computationally accessible without adding raw information.
4. **Local learning with an explicit cross-producer boundary.** Reuse own-write eligibility for persistent receiver parameters and read-time credit for addressing. Reuse native recursive event/mark/silence likelihoods as candidate local objectives. The latter have connectivity/algebra evidence, not demonstrated whole-task superiority. Predictable codes can be irrelevant or collapsed; stop-gradient alone does not prevent that. Require task-relevant observable targets or measured task utility. Return version-addressed one-hop producer credit only after its dependency, stale-version and derivative contracts pass; do not call an inbox an implemented writer update.
5. **Joint supervision must be sufficient for its claimed job.** Independent identity recognition and single-child swap discrimination need not teach a whole relation. The existing binding race teacher is gated but has no acquired quality result and no joint-sufficiency guarantee. Choose its role from the actual task algebra before fitting it. Avoid latent oracle labels.
6. **Scale the strongest integrated candidate.** Validate on the actual eight-layer token interface and existing acquired hierarchy, with complete state/history/credit and resource declarations. Include causal multi-memory reads, historical-version stability, joint content/role sensitivity, finite task-facing credit and serialized recovery. Numerical gates decide correctness only. A predeclared development acquisition must judge whole prediction and local relational learning, disclose data/coverage, and retain exact checkpoints for justified continuation. No new dense controls or ablation grid.

## Current integration gap

`LocalNativeDeepLM.stream_nll` retrieves old depth views in the prediction head and feeds outcome-matured cause inboxes into the next event. `PackedLocalDeepRace.consume` itself receives the current lower message, detaches at layer boundaries, and updates its own selected receiver. It does not call the hierarchy's lower-memory query/binding interfaces or its recursive bound-pool teacher. Consequently the current run is a native persistent local-learning foundation, not the completed architecture described above. Preserve it unchanged, report its actual score, and integrate from the existing capability map before another acquisition.
