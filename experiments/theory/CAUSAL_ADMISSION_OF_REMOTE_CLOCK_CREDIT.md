# Causal admission of remote clock credit

13 October 2026. B10 design after completed16,384forecast continuation. Own-leg credit beats exact BPTT by0.000970nats/event; unconditional four-direction remote correction gains0.000764 versus BPTT but loses0.000205 versus own-leg. Remote correction helps the first two quarters and hurts the last two. These observed intervals motivate learning admission; they do not prove prospective predictability or identify the cause of the changing benefit.

Keep the verified own-leg contribution as baseline g_own. Let R_t be the completed shared-path correction for the current32event window. A causal controller emits an admission a_t in[0,1] from information available before the update, yielding g_t=g_own+a_t R_t. Initially a_t=0 protects the stronger measured rule. Endpoint a_t=1 recovers the already tested projected rule. A scalar gate is an initial diagnostic; route/leg-specific gates can retain different shared effects when justified by learned context.

The admission model should reuse the native event-race family, addressed memory and delayed event feedback. Represent producer/leg identity together with incoming state, time, parameter version and observed error; sharing an address alone does not imply shared utility. Store controller activations and the chosen correction for subsequent teaching. No GRU or disconnected dense gradient-estimator substitution is needed.

The teaching target is subsequent predictive utility under the actual optimizer, not exact historical-gradient agreement. At the update boundary, form own-only and admitted actual Adam proposals with the same old moments/clipping and numerical memory. After future observations arrive, replay both parameter proposals over the same admitted future observation horizon, including each proposal's own state evolution. Compare recording-cell forecast losses. The native controller learns from this delayed difference or its differentiable actual-update utility. A baseline penalty on admission can charge risk and work. Teacher evaluations, cloned states, optimizer proposals and controller updates are all charged.

For this forecasting benchmark, changing predictor weights does not change the recorded market events, so comparing weight interventions against the same observations has a well-defined prediction objective. It is not an intervention on trader behavior, a profit estimator, or evidence about a policy whose market actions change subsequent data. Do not treat the realized future as known when choosing the earlier gate.

Chronology: score the current event with the existing actor; observe its outcome; accumulate the completed window; choose admission from current/past information; apply an actual update; gather the next window's score-before-update outcomes; only then teach the stored admission activation. Detach/reset actor sensitivities after updates. If a teacher is intermittent, record its event-time horizon and parameter version; do not claim unobserved utility targets for skipped windows.

An a=0 endpoint preserves the own-leg raw update before controller side effects. It does not guarantee non-worsening learned decisions: finite-sample utility estimates, distribution change and gate optimization can select harmful admission. Compare own-only, fixed full admission, acquired-frozen native gate and online native gate with identical actor initialization, numerical state and acquired optimizer/controller states. Freeze only controller parameters in the frozen arm; its native state can continue receiving causal events. Require gates to earn their prediction quality AND full cost. Native feedback and alternative exploration should share common legs rather than duplicate a dense response model.

For economical deployment, choose/admit transport at a window boundary from earlier context; computing R_t and then declining it saves no tangent work. In the first information experiment always computing R_t can isolate gate utility, but cannot establish activity savings. A later admission-before-window schedule must compare the same controller information opportunities and reset a newly admitted sensitivity bank correctly; a new basis cannot reconstruct past sensitivities without charged replay.

Implemented first information experiment (13 October): NativeMarketCredit retains its connected hard-race/addressed/history body with a scalar utility head,31,753parameters. It stores the current decision activation until the next observed window trains it against two actualAdam forecast proposals. Native numerical memory persists within the episode, but its graph is detached between controller calls; the delayed loss trains the stored decision frame, not all historical controller-memory writes. Actor and controller banks remain separate. Both endpoint gradients are obtained in one local sweep with exact parity to their frozen separate implementations. Current deployment still computes remote sensitivities before admission, so rejected corrections save no tangent work. Source actor acquisition stays exactly on the earlier BPTT trajectory; native acquisition adds future-utility teaching without altering those actor updates. These boundaries keep the partial construction distinct from the complete dual-learning/shared-bank objective.
