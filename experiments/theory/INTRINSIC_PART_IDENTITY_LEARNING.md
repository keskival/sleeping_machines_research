# Learn part identity before asking upper memories to compose it

16 October2026. One integrated native acquisition;no new ablation arms.

The current evidence identifies three different capabilities. Geometry
reconstruction improves34.6% without class transfer. Versioned part access
preserves recoverable bindings without changing the acquired actor. Observed
ownership fixes a measured25%support defect and supplies eight complete part
memories. None guarantees that a parent receives a reusable identity code.

For an observed component P,define C(P) as its sorted relative pixel positions
and polarities after subtracting its observed bounding origin. This signature
is invariant to translation,time origin and event order;changes of internal
shape or polarity remain distinguishable. Fit the finite vocabulary of C(P)
from TRAIN observations only. This uses neither the generator's latent part
identities nor its arrangement bits. The current fixture gives eight shapes.

The student predicts that observed identity from its **native level-one part
memory**. It never receives the identity as input. At inference the classifier
head and target lookup are unused;pixel-native state,part receipt races,
role-bearing messages and all higher native stages perform the prediction.
The targets anchor identity against teacher-code collapse while leaving the
rest of the native vector free to carry timing and contextual information.
This is supervised self-prediction of observable shape,not a learned universal
visual taxonomy or a semantic codebook discovery claim.

Identity alone cannot solve the whole label:every scene contains the same set
of parts. The label depends on their arrangement. Thus local identity teaching
cannot supply a class shortcut;upper memories must bind identities to roles
and learn nonadditive relations. Low identity error is a necessary useful
intermediate capability,not sufficient evidence for whole-label transfer.

The single admitted learner minimizes whole-label cross entropy plus0.25times
mean part-identity cross entropy. The weight is fixed before acquisition;no
DEV sweep or new control fit. It reuses the strongest acquired original role
actor inside corrected ownership,with8levels/32native stages,counterfactual
receipt learning,temporal transport and timer-generated views. The identity
head adds264trainable parameters for32channels/eight shapes. Bound part
versions remain addressable;ownership and query/arming policies are explicit.
Recursive likelihood heads are implemented separately but are not active in
this acquisition. No mechanism is claimed merely because it exists elsewhere.

Twenty-one combined numerical contracts pass,including unchanged inference
when adding the teacher,observable-target invariance and credit into the real
native part memory. An actual paired optimizer/resource gate precedes one
64paired-update acquisition. Save model,Adam,RNG,order,source/input hashes and
initial features every8updates;state persists within each independent scene.
A TRAIN-only ridge0.01root readout scores the same reused64scene DEV protocol.
The existing acquired reference0.686609is reused;no new ablation acquisition or
TEST access. Full native/teacher reads,candidate and counterfactual work are

Read the result in two dimensions:does identity become more readable across
nuisances,and does whole-label prediction improve? Success in the first only
means the next bottleneck is higher relation learning;it is not permission to
repeat unchanged shape training or declare a semantic win. Whole-label gains
are within-family development evidence until independently confirmed.
