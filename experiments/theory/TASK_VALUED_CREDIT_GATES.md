## Economical utility audits: inspect policy differences, not every route error

Let q be the true conditional future task cost under a declared actor/learning policy, qhat its model, and p0,p two candidate route distributions frozen before future feedback. Write d=p0−p, L=sum|d|. The estimated gain is Ghat=sum d qhat; true gain G=sum d q. If max|q−qhat|<=epsilon on changed support, then |G−Ghat|<=epsilon L. Thus Ghat>epsilon L is a sufficient improvement certificate under that error premise. For unchanged policies L=0, no route-cost audit is needed to compare them. This is a conditional bound, not a measured guarantee from regression MSE. A common action-independent error cancels exactly because sum d=0; isolated component error need not cancel after a route change.

For L>0 sample a paid intervention A with r(a)=|d(a)|/L on d!=0, and use

    Gsample = sum_a d(a) qhat(a) + d(A)/r(A) * [Y_A−qhat(A)].

Given the issued packet/policies, E[Y_A|A=a]=q(a) yields E[Gsample]=G, even for a biased qhat. One-sample variance is L^2 E_r[(Y_A−qhat(A))^2]−[sum d(q−qhat)]^2. With n conditionally independent samples, divide by n. For two distinct deterministic routes L=2 and only those two interventions need supported comparison; unrelated candidate errors need no audit for that particular decision. With correlated continuations, the independent-sample variance formula does not apply. Known sampling probabilities and positive support wherever d!=0 are essential; policy selection using those same audited outcomes would invalidate this frozen-policy calculation. Fitting predictions need not be unbiased because the supported residual is what repairs the mean.

This suggests a causal sparse implementation: issue candidate policy and versioned prediction first, schedule supported residual checks only on changed routes, and use revealed paired outcomes to train the next critic/version. Charge the existing candidate discovery/query and prediction sum; this formula does not make an already dense candidate table free. For implicit large route universes, obtaining p and the prediction sum requires its own sparse policy/importance construction. Nonstationary q_t is allowed conditional on the current history and declared horizon; stale outcome labels target a different q and do not inherit this guarantee. Investor-facing scaling evidence still requires integrated future utility and whole-work measurements.
