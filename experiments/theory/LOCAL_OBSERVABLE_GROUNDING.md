strongest path, without attachment to proposed mechanisms.

## Evidence selects the actor and the failure to address

The matched additional64-update comparison gives materialized DEV NLL0.686609
for continued native role-memory versus0.716414 for the carried-view actor.
The carrier's2.73–9.30x local sensitivity gate did not predict a quality gain.
Do not promote it on gradient magnitude. The original native role-memory actor
is the stronger acquired candidate; uncertainty and emitted-channel attribution
are scored separately. Output responsibility allocation is a working access
and credit diagnostic, not the primary model: its pooled views lose spatial
bindings, and rising gate depth is not useful semantic hierarchy.

We have been giving many parents no learning job except transmitting a small
whole-label signal through a long nonlinear path. On the parity task individual
children have no whole-label marginal signal. Direct isolated-child class losses
would therefore fail by design. The selected next hypothesis is to ground every
local representation in its own revealed observation support, while retaining
the native cross-child role composition and whole-label objective.

## A fixed observable teacher avoids moving-target collapse

For revealed coordinates x_i in a parent's support, let b be its observed support
origin. Use fixed Fourier directions omega_k and separate polarity channels:

    target_(sign,k) = sum_{i:sign_i=sign} [cos,sin](omega_k dot (x_i-b)) / sqrt(N).

The zero direction carries signed counts. Seven other fixed directions preserve
finer position-dependent information. This32-dimensional target uses actual
pixel displacements, not quantized coarse occupancy that aliases small shapes.
It ignores nuisance timestamp permutations for this static geometric task;
future event prediction needs separately defined temporal targets. The sketch
is not proven sufficient for every geometry or the label. Finite projection,
superposition and nuisance choices can lose information and must be audited.

A level-specific training-only linear decoder predicts this target from the
actual native published memory read. Targets depend on sensor rows and declared
support geometry only: no part IDs, latent tree codes, labels, future observations
or learned teacher weights. Targets never enter actor computation. Equal loss
weight per active level, after averaging within that level, prevents many pixel
objects from defining the entire objective.

For a constant representation and its best constant decoder, squared loss is
the target variance (averaged over channels). A nonconstant fixed target therefore
penalizes collapse; a moving learned target could instead collapse alongside
the encoder. This is an objective property, not a convergence guarantee: a
collapsed zero decoder can still be stationary. Nonzero ordinary decoder
initialization provides live local derivatives, checked in the native model.

Fit objective candidate: whole-label CE +0.1local reconstruction. The coefficient
is a single declared gate setting, not a tuning campaign. Gradient norms and
cosines with whole CE are recorded to reveal destructive conflicts before a
long fit. Decoder fitting alone is not representation improvement: a later
comparison must demonstrate encoder changes, held-out reconstruction and task
transfer, with frozen-encoder and training-only calibration controls as needed.

## Mechanisms and accounting

GroundedNativeComposition inherits the stronger RelationalFrameComposition.
It preserves all32native temporal stages, native receipt/update races, persistent
indexed state, role-conditioned and ordered cross-child paths, keys/values,
local counterfactual teacher and race-generated views. Native predictions are
bitwise identical after checkpoint migration. The geometry teacher/decoders
are unused at inference. They add training work, parameters and optimizer state;
count indexed reads, target row tests, phases/trigonometric evaluations and
decoder MAdds. Full backward/native arithmetic remains a separate audit.

First admission is one real TRAIN pair, not a quality fit. Load the completed
continued-relational raw actor, measure local/whole credit and conflicts, run
one optimizer update with watchdogs. Existing frozen endpoint attribution
finishes unchanged. Admit a quality comparison only after this gate and review
of the completed comparison. No repeated carrier fit or output-routing fit is
automatically admitted. Learned part ownership and content-sufficient messages
remain capabilities to build, not conclusions from geometric reconstruction.
