## Why common time and branch credit fit together

The race law can equivalently be sampled as E~Exp(1), T=E/Lambda and J~Categorical(pi), independently conditional on prefix. For a frozen-parameter local continuation loss L_j(T,theta), the expected differential contains continuous factual terms, the common-time term with dT/ds_i=-T*pi_i, and the discrete term sum_j L_j(T)*d pi_j. Common producer time does not imply common downstream cotangents: partial_T L_j can depend on branch and continuation.

The source temporal surrogate first_s=first-first*(log(Lambda)-stopgrad(log(Lambda))) implements the factorized common-time derivative. It is not the derivative of min_j E_j/exp(s_j) holding every original individual E_j fixed. With the declared fixed-time categorical intervention, a factual winner w and positive-support alternative proposal q give

    E_a[(L_a(T)-L_w(T))*d pi_a/q_a | T,w] = sum_j L_j(T)*d pi_j,

because sum_j d pi_j=0. Thus keeping the common clock while forcing the alternative write is a coherent factorized race intervention, not a claim that the alternative won at its own original individual arrival. The current teacher uses a bounded retained-state suffix, not an infinite/full-history task objective. A nonlinear optimizer's future utility is a separate teaching target.

Unchanged prefixes reuse physical state and RNG exactly; branch continuations need their own downstream responses. This gives a concrete shared-leg boundary: cache the shared primal computation, sum or learn appropriately conditioned cotangents, and teach branch residuals from actual forward outcomes/paid corrections. No unbounded hierarchy of trainers is needed.
