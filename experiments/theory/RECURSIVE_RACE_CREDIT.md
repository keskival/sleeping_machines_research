# Recursive prediction needs an exposure ledger and informative bindings

16 October 2026. Native integration contracts; no trained quality claim.

The completed geometry experiment changes the design objective. An additive
sketch can preserve more scene information (34.6% less calibrated root error)
without making a seven-relation label readable. A higher level must model how
identities bind to roles, not merely recover a sum of lower vectors. The Curie
4M-token result supplies complementary evidence: L8 improves word-initial NLL
6.2988 → 6.2695; part-whole levels carry unique predictive information. Neither
result by itself proves reusable semantic identities.

## Exact local credit with native races

Reuse the existing `RaceReadout` competing defective log-normal clocks. For a
forecast issued from published lower memories, let S_j(t-r_j) be clock j's
survival and h_j(c,t) its marked hazard. On one interval (u,v],

    log p(c,v | no event through u)
      = sum_j [log S_j(v-r_j)-log S_j(u-r_j)]
        + log sum_j h_j(c,v).

A query q between u and v contributes only log S(q)-log S(u), then advances the
exposure cursor to q. The event contributes survival from q onward. These terms
telescope, including their gradients. Charging a fresh full-gap likelihood at
every query would multiply silent evidence by the query frequency. Reading a
memory is not observing a new event. A right-censored episode contributes only
its uncharged survival interval; it must not invent an emission.

`recursive_race_learning.py` connects this objective to native **published**
indexed memories at every level. Existing temporal transport, native selected
writes/races/timers, keys/values and counterfactual machinery are preserved.
The adapter predicts from an explicitly supplied pool, retains its immutable
forecast tensors, and sends credit through live lower-memory reads. Auxiliary
heads do not change actor predictions or publish events. The adapter is not yet
a learned concept assignment/emission policy or a trained hierarchy.

Availability time is the observable clock for computation-generated events.
Physical sensor time is separate metadata. The present point-density contract
assumes a continuous non-atomic emission clock; a recording-cell likelihood is
required before applying it to tied/discretized concept streams. Updating a
forecast requires explicitly closing/accounting the old exposure before issuing
a new law. Do not change parameters in place before backpropagating its pending
loss graph; exact forward snapshots are not permission to reuse invalid graphs.

## What recursive likelihood does and does not guarantee

Stop-gradient at code labels prevents the *upper loss's direct* incentive to
collapse the lower codes. It does not prevent collapse from lower training,
poor initialization, EMA drift or redundant code use. Slow codebooks are a
stability device, not evidence of semantic identity. Store codebook versions or
key snapshots with residual payloads: decoding an old residual with a changed
key no longer reproduces the issued whole.

For log-loss in nats, the Bayes benefit of additional state U beyond Z equals
I(Y;U | Z) when unrestricted optimal decoders use the same information boundary.
Finite decoders and optimization need not realize that benefit. Better local
likelihood is not proof of positive task information: an irrelevant predictable
stream can improve indefinitely. Nor does type closure transfer empirical
benchmark wins automatically. Closure preserves causal interfaces; estimation,
clock law, stable identities, task relevance and useful credit must be tested.

The next semantic admission must measure (1) identity stability under observed
nuisance, (2) sensitivity to role changes with parts held fixed, (3) predictive
value of cross-part interaction beyond single-part/additive controls, and (4)
whole-label transfer against the acquired frozen actor. Use sensor observations
and TRAIN labels only; no latent task identities. Retain the existing geometry
gain as an independent capability. Do not spend another acquisition on an
auxiliary objective that can win while ignoring the required relation.

## Executable scope and costs

Contracts cover partition-invariant likelihood **and parameter gradients**,
zero duplicate-query exposure, censoring without fabricated marks, invalid-label
rejection, and live four-level native integration: every level receives credit
while published values, versions, times, event counts and timers remain unchanged.
Pools charge their reads, candidate slots, mark logits and affine work. Address
discovery, transport, nonlinear/special functions, backward/optimizer work and
native actor work remain separate; no complete FLOP or hardware claim.
