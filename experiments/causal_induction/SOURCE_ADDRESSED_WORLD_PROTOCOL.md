## Mechanism and limits

The existing native world has one target-addressed memory. The child retains its parameters, temporal races, shared-bank read/write policies, outcome teaching, separate keys/values, factual actor differentiation and sampled alternative credit. Its only architectural intervention is the numerical destination of world state: (target, latest publicly observed exogenous source at issue time). Feedback restores that issued pair instead of using a newer source. No hidden graph, learned address embedding, new dense recurrent model or chosen-route conditioning is introduced.

The source event is a public reference, not proof of a hidden causal edge. The world posterior still conditions on a common issue-time observable independent of route draws; its proper-loss projections retain the common-belief formulation. Target/source associations can now accumulate across delayed queries with that pair. More visited pairs increase memory and discovery work; all costs must be reported. The proposal does not guarantee decoder improvement: independent pair states may lose useful sharing or fragment already scarce feedback.
