## Extraction gaps from existing runs

- Stage-by-stage content/timing interventions are not completed for the AWS deep fits. Every-layer gradient and occupancy logs do not identify useful information transport.
- Best-weight checkpoints exist at intermediate acquired sizes, but complete intermediate learner cuts (optimizer/allocator/numerical state/RNG) are not all retained. Final numerical cuts support replay; old weight-only checkpoints cannot be relabelled as complete future learners.
- Long-history quality and vocabulary buckets are evaluated, but quality gained per complete work, plateau/reversal locations and stage-demand distributions need a unified matched-view analysis.
- The successful richerD2/8M reference is not yet included in every deep evaluation. Existing source-frozen scorer results remain valid; add the stronger reference in a new source-bound analysis rather than editing prior evidence.
- No native fitted horizon critic yet. The correct learner-consequence infrastructure makes that acquisition possible, not already successful.

## Better evidence yield per expensive acquisition

Before the next long fit, register its decision, matched data/quality/work boundaries, diagnostic hypotheses and reusable artifacts. Capture versioned stage/route summaries at the existing evaluation cadence without changing factual RNG. Record clipping activation and local-update distributions for the local/global comparison; charge instrumentation, and avoid logging every event unnecessarily. Save a bounded number of complete acquisition-stage learner cuts prospectively, not only selected weight checkpoints.

After completion: paired frozen development scoring with strongest saved compatible references; frequency/history/stage diagnostics; whole-fitting and per-target work together; diagnosis distinguishing protocol, numerical, representation, learning and acquisition explanations. Prefer cheap inference and bounded replay on saved checkpoints to another fit. A loss changes the next hypothesis, not the interpretation of unrelated positive evidence. New TEST selection is not authorized by the desire for more diagnostics.
