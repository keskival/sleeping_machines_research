# B10: chronological credit adaptation

The shuffled Taxi fits do not test causal changes in predictive relationships. The first market continuation isolates credit adaptation using a shared acquisition on the first 2,048 scored August 1 TRAIN events, followed by 4,096 chronological events. All four arms inherit exactly the acquired actor, optimizer moments, numerical forward and recurrent credit state. Online credit updates its parameters and trust calibration from revealed audits; frozen credit freezes both while its activations evolve. Zero-prediction residual audits and exact native truncated BPTT isolate ordinary online learning. All arms update the same native actor with the same AdamW learning rate and feedback grouping.

Forecasts are issued before outcomes and never rescored after an update. Producer graphs are consumed under their original parameter version; numerical state persists, derivatives truncate at 32-event feedback boundaries. Recording-cell likelihood, clocks, addressed mark values and silence supervision are retained. This v5 member uses dense local derivatives and boundary credit, not optional hard-route counterfactual credit. The predictor uses feedback-conditioned recurrent state with one paid update per audit; no expensive full producer Gram or replay.

Decision: retain adapting credit only if future prequential NLL improves against frozen credit and zero-audit, and compare complete acquisition plus reuse cost against native BPTT. A real chronological gain alone does not prove changing inductive biases. The next causal diagnostic must hide a change in conditional relationships from every learner, distinguish it from marginal drift, and measure recovery with shared pre-change initialization. No September TEST or validation selection is used here. One seed is development evidence.
