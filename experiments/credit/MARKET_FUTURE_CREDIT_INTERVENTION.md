# B10: train credit interventions for future utility

Chronological cotangent imitation and hidden dependency-reversal tests both fail to improve over frozen credit. Audited cotangent MSE improves slightly on the real fragment while task utility falls. The next change addresses that objective mismatch directly.

Retain the native temporal actor, persistent state, local factual gradients and residual audit rule. Add a small feedback-conditioned intervention policy. It samples z ~ Normal(mu(packet), .5²) and multiplies predicted boundary credit by 2 sigmoid(z). The independent .25-probability audit still corrects the residual; unbiasedness holds before actor clipping/Adam, not after that nonlinear update. Different multipliers can affect realized variance and future utility.

After the next 32 forecasts have been issued and scored, teach the previous sampled intervention using (actual next-window NLL - reference next-window NLL) times log probability of the sample. The reference replays those revealed observations using parameters from before the previous actor update and the same carried numerical state. It is independent of that intervention sample. This is a one-window direct parameter-update objective conditional on the held carried state, not the total long-horizon causal effect including state interventions. All model clones, reference replay, policy inference and optimization are charged.

All arms share acquisition. During reuse the base cotangent critic and trust calibration are frozen; online adapts only the intervention policy. Frozen policy, zero-prediction residual audits and native BPTT give the controls. Audit draws are matched by a separate seeded RNG. Gaussian interventions have the same fixed variance but their innovations are not paired across arms; this first development run is not a paired-seed confirmation. Smoke checks finite scores and critic/frozen-policy immutability, then the same 2,048-acquisition/4,096-continuation TRAIN fragment. No TEST or benchmark claim; a scalar policy is an initial utility integration, not the proposed expressive shared critic's capacity limit.
