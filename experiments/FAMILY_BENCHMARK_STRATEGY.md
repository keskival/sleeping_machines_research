## The consequence of the definition

Sleeping Machines is a causal network of stateful temporal programs, with
declared evidence, operators, state, interfaces, participation, scheduling,
queries, objective, learner, bounds and execution. A uniform winner-only
receiver stack is one member. Selective state maintenance can compose with
exact reception, historical retrieval, explicit statistics or a dense query
region when the interfaces preserve the required information and credit.

The winning research policy is therefore to find the earliest information,
precision, access or credit failure and repair that region. Increasing every
layer's width/depth/pool is a costly, poorly identified intervention. A local
rich region is a legitimate family member; its dense work must be counted and
its success attributed to the mechanisms actually used. A dense control alone
does not demonstrate capacity beyond selected temporal activity.

The documentation distinguishes expressivity, useful learning and economic
benefit. We need all three for an integrated advantage, and may achieve a
public quality foothold before a complete resource win. The broad containment
construction removes a blanket event/sparsity representational ceiling; it
does not give the currently trained narrow member a Transformer's full function.

## Expected reception is a concrete expansion with a remaining write boundary

The implemented `expected_reception.py` computes

    value = sum_u softmax(score)_u * proposal_u
    common first time = 1/sum_u exp(score_u)
    committed memory address = argmax score.

It keeps temporal flow, separate keys/values, persistent private evidence and
one hard write per head. It removes current value sampling and clock sampling,
but computes every proposal at inference. Its value/clock program is smooth
inside the ordinary numerical domain and fixed write history; **the full
recurrent member is not globally smooth at argmax write boundaries**.
Exact current reception is not the expectation of the complete stochastic
trajectory. Mean unbounded first time also does not equal mean native bounded
delay. These qualifications leave the implementation useful and testable.

For MG, compare expected reception to sampled/mix8/argmax on matched DEV fits;
pool1 remains a diagnostic rather than the integrated multi-alternative target.
A useful reduction from eight sampled streams to one all-proposal stream can
improve precision and perhaps serving cost, but must be measured: U proposals,
all keys and state traffic are paid. An accuracy gain with dense reception can
motivate a later smaller shortlist/multi-delivery member, provided its missing
mass and nonlinear rollout error are checked. Do not sparsify first and assume
the exact-reception quality survives.

For primate and speech, richer reception can combine complementary evidence.
It may also average away useful specialized state or increase fitting work.
Use the existing sparse parent, a fixed richer final-stage member, and an
all-layer richer diagnostic to locate the effect. These are prospective
comparisons, not assigned additional training before the owned DEV arms finish.

## Train the consequence that the task needs

The default local teacher teaches delivered message utility. It does not
generally teach which private write improves later predictions. Expected
reception gives exact derivatives of its current pooled value, but hard write
addresses still lack complete future utility. More proposals or smoother
delivery cannot alone repair this learning gap.

For deeper language models, actual replay should keep the same causal prefix,
forked noise/state and full specified return. Inspect raw gradients, clipping,
warm Adam and function changes, not only a norm or local unbiasedness claim.
Prioritize the existing depth8 replay/teacher comparison; distinguish its
streaming chronology from cold segment-batched fits. Residual control variates
and sampled site allocation can reduce expensive alternative work only with
positive support, honest anchor cost and unchanged target utility. Longer
horizon should be justified by useful writer credit, not retention alone.

For optional reception/depth later, the return must include every fixed query,
fallback, future state and full incremental resource cost. Silence is an
internal observation/decision; it never licenses omission of difficult scored
targets. The twelve-field specification should accompany each new member.

## How to establish and present an advantage

1. Freeze the task, inputs, queries, split, selection rule, repeated fits,
   inference policy and resource boundary before confirmation.
2. Contract the relevant retained state/timing/gradient/update/recovery paths.
   A changed member needs its own contracts; illustrative definitions are not tests.
3. Run a small integrated DEV comparison to diagnose the intended failure.
   Preserve the successful parent, failed controls and research/search cost.
4. Confirm quality on the complete official workload. Report seed dispersion
   and session/repeat coverage honestly, plus any previously exposed portion.
5. Verify trained serving parity, then show a quality/resource frontier:
   whole-fit GFLOPs and MFLOPs per presented target together, inference work,
   latency/state footprint, and measured traffic/energy when available.

A public quality win, a better resource tradeoff and broad supremacy have
different evidential burdens. Lead a report with the strongest completed
appropriate comparison and its actual mechanism profile. Show pending/negative
results beside it. Do not describe the conditional attention aggregation
opportunity as a measured half-cost full Transformer.
