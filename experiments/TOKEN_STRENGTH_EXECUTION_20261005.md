# Language advantage through the substrate's own strengths

5 October 2026 · User-directed continuation · CPU-only, GPT-2-tokenized FineWeb.

Sleeping Machines targets a general-purpose substrate spanning language and reasoning,
multimodal world models, embodiment, event-native analytics, continual learning,
communication, self-design and hardware. Language is the current decisive evidence front.

The benchmark program deliberately targets the data region where strong Transformer
references lead LSTMs. The primary opponent is that Transformer. Tiny native fits
are engineering diagnostics before the target-scale comparison, not a change of
benchmark objective. Reuse published recurrent evidence rather than a new LSTM grid.

## Working conviction and selection rule

The strongest route is useful persistent event memory with sparse addressed activity,
trained through the actual future consequences of hard choices. Preserve temporal races,
computational delays, separate keys/values, deep persistent representations and small
messages. Architecture and execution need not imitate Transformer layers, fixed windows,
or dense processing. Derivation, numerical contracts and integrated held-out results
select the implementation; conviction does not supply benchmark scores.

Allow our numerical state to survive for its useful lifetime, allow available capacity
to grow independently of selected writes, and measure complete learning cost rather
than limiting counterfactual learning to the factual inference graph. Optional depth,
asynchronous schedules and continual adaptation are legitimate family capabilities;
introduce each after a measured failure and a contracted integrated comparison.

Do not handicap native state to a Transformer window in every experiment. Do not
call a longer-context result matched-history. Shared output targets and causal data
access protect the conclusion without prescribing internal computation.
