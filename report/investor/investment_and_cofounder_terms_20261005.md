# Sleeping Machines — proposed investment and cofounder terms

**Discussion proposal · 5 October 2026 · EUR**

Sleeping Machines is developing a general-purpose computational substrate spanning language and reasoning, multimodal world models, embodiment, event-native analytics, continual learning, communication, self-design and hardware. Its architecture computes through time and sparse asynchronous events, combining persistent representations with useful capacity beyond active work.

We invite prospective angel investors who can also take an active cofounder role to discuss the following terms. Cash investment and ongoing founder contribution are valued separately, with both reflected transparently in the final ownership structure.

## 1. Cash investment

| Term | Proposal |
|---|---|
| Pre-money equity valuation | **€5,000,000** |
| Target aggregate new investment | **€250,000–€500,000** |
| Financing basis | A priced equity investment into the company |
| Investor allocation | Proportional to each investor's cash contribution at the agreed share price |
| Use of funds | Founder runway, research and engineering, compute, reproducible validation and initial commercial development |

The €5 million valuation is the proposed value of the company's equity immediately before this financing. New cash increases the post-money valuation.

**Cash investor ownership = investment ÷ (pre-money valuation + total new cash investment).**

The illustrations below assume the indicated amount is the entire round. They exclude additional founder grants, option-pool changes, existing convertible instruments and other simultaneous issuances.

| Total cash raised | Pre-money valuation | Post-money valuation | Aggregate ownership bought with cash |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| €250,000 | €5,000,000 | €5,250,000 | 4.76% |
| €500,000 | €5,000,000 | €5,500,000 | 9.09% |

If several investors participate, they divide the aggregate cash-investor allocation in proportion to their investments. For example, two investors contributing €250,000 each in a €500,000 round each receive approximately **4.55%** from their cash investment alone, under these assumptions.

This proposal uses a priced pre-money valuation. A SAFE or convertible instrument would require a separately agreed structure and conversion terms; a valuation cap is not interchangeable with this priced valuation.

## 2. Active cofounder contribution

An investor joining as an active cofounder may receive a separate equity allocation for their work and long-term commitment. The allocation will be agreed individually, taking account of:

- The role and responsibility assumed, including technical development, company leadership, commercial development or fundraising.
- Full-time or part-time commitment, start date and expected duration.
- Relevant expertise, execution ability and contribution to building the company.
- Salary arrangements and the personal risk undertaken.

**No additional cofounder percentage is fixed in this proposal.** That percentage requires agreement on the actual role and commitment. Cash investment alone does not determine the additional founder allocation.

The proposed starting point for service equity is **four-year vesting with a one-year cliff**, beginning on the agreed service start date: 25% vests after twelve months of qualifying service, with the remainder vesting monthly over the following thirty-six months. Departure treatment and any acceleration provisions will be agreed in the definitive documents.

Equity purchased for cash is separate from service equity and is not subject to this service-vesting schedule under the proposal.

## 3. Combined ownership and dilution

Each participant's final ownership will reflect both their cash-purchased shares and any agreed service-equity allocation. The definitive capitalization table must show:

- Existing ownership before the financing.
- All cash investments and resulting share allocations.
- Any cofounder service-equity grants, their vesting and their percentage basis.
- Any employee option pool or other reserved equity.
- Fully diluted ownership immediately after all agreed transactions.

Service-equity percentages must specify whether they are calculated before or after the financing and option-pool changes. The cash-only illustrations above are not promises of final combined ownership. Future share issuances may dilute existing shareholders.

## 4. Funding objectives

The initial capital is intended to advance the existing research and implementation into a scalable, reproducibly validated platform. Priorities include integrated model learning and capacity utilization, larger tokenized language comparisons against published Transformer evidence, resource measurement, and selection of an initial commercial application.

The investment case combines the architecture's broad ambition, the implementation and evidence already developed, and the team's ability to execute the next validation and commercialization milestones. Specific technical results and their experimental scope will be supplied in the accompanying research materials.

## 5. Matters to agree before completion

The parties will agree the company's jurisdiction and legal entity, share class and associated rights, governance and decision-making, founder roles and remuneration, service-equity allocations, intellectual-property ownership and licensing, and the final capitalization table. Investment timing and any closing conditions will also be documented.

The initial discussion should establish each participant's proposed cash contribution, working role, time commitment and availability, then produce one combined capitalization table for review.

## 6. Status of this proposal

This document is a **non-binding proposal for discussion**. It is not an offer acceptance, investment commitment or final shareholder agreement. Investment and cofounder arrangements take effect only through the definitive agreements approved and executed by the relevant parties.
