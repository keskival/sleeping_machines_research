## Recommended business structure

Protect separable technical choke points: event prediction, persistent binding memory, learning machinery and execution machinery. Do not file one enormous “general intelligence” claim or one application per experiment. A **family** here means a proposed common disclosure/priority group; it is not a granted patent, and related claim groups may need separate applications or divisionals. EP unity depends on common special technical features, not common branding ([EPO unity guidance](https://www.epo.org/en/legal/guidelines-epc/2026/f_v_2.html)).

I recommend seven first-wave family disclosures: **PF-01, PF-02, PF-03, PF-04, PF-06, PF-08 and PF-09**. PF-01 is the separately handled earlier-public US candidate; the other six cover current product and learning engines, subject to the feature-level disclosure audit. Draft PF-07 alongside PF-06, but use a separate filing only if posterior-credit claims survive prior art and merit the extra prosecution. PF-05 is a sensor-product filing when that product has a committed buyer/licensee. PF-10, PF-11 and PF-12 are reserve disclosures for growth, hardware/compiler and output-head embodiments; assess their priority support promptly, even though demonstrated commercial advantages are less mature. Filing order is not permission to delay publication-sensitive protection. Counsel can lodge multiple fully supported disclosures in a coordinated priority package, then choose subsequent distinct claim sets; a combined provisional does not ensure unity, novelty or priority support for later claims.

Keep PF-02 state clocks with the heterogeneous clock engine: same forecast product, overlapping evidence and a natural shared race/survival mechanism. Keep PF-03 separate because recording-resolution protection can be licensed across clock architectures. Keep predecessor-key memory, read-time episodic credit and anonymous duration binding in PF-04's common technical disclosure; the latter is a potential industrial divisional if unity requires it. Separate exact traces (PF-06), posterior credit (PF-07), learner-world teaching (PF-08) and update delivery (PF-09): they are usable independently, face different prior art and have different buyers. Don't force all of old IDF-08 into one independent claim.
